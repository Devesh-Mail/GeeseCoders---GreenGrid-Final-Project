How matching works

Exact expertise phrase scores 10, a related term scores 5, then availability adds up to 3 and a strong rating adds 1. Availability is only ever a tiebreak — a well-matched mentor with one slot still outranks a poor match with ten. It's deterministic, so the same question always returns the same order, and every card tells you what it matched on.
